**A List is where your prospects live.** You can fill it from any combination of sources — Guhan's daily discovery search, buying-signal triggers, competitor engagement, another List, a CSV upload, or by hand. A Campaign then works the List — messaging every prospect through your chosen sequence.

In the SDR workflow, Lists cover *find fit prospects* + *research each one* (Steps 2-3). A prospect landing in a List has already been researched by Guhan (see the automatic research brief on every prospect card). Campaigns then take over for the drafting + outreach steps.

Lists let you separate audiences. Common patterns:

- **One List per persona** — "Enterprise VPs", "SMB Owners", "RevOps Managers"
- **One List per market** — "US SaaS", "EU Fintech"
- **One List per campaign** — "Q1 launch outreach", "Conference follow-up"

## How prospects get in

A List can have zero, one, or many **sources**. Each source is a way of feeding new prospects into the List.

<Tip>
  Most Guhan users combine two or three sources on the same List. Example: a "search criteria" source (VP Sales at SaaS companies) plus a "funding raised recently" signal source — the List fills with matching people at recently-funded companies, and every prospect carries clear signal context the message can hook onto.
</Tip>

## How prospects get out

- **Automatically** — Guhan removes prospects who reply "not interested", who go on Do Not Contact, or whose LinkedIn account gets restricted.
- **Manually** — remove prospects one at a time or in bulk from the List detail page.
- **After enrollment** — a prospect enrolled in an Campaign stays in the List; enrolling doesn't remove them.

## The List detail page

Every List has a detail page with:

- **Prospect table** — every prospect in this List, filterable + searchable + exportable. Each row shows the fit chip (Strong / Moderate / Weak) from [how Guhan scores prospects](/audience/lists/how-scoring-works).
- **Sources panel** — every source feeding this List, with per-source counts and edit affordances.
- **Sweep history** — the last 30 discovery runs, drillable into per-prospect detail.
- **Bound Campaign** — which agent works this List.

## Daily rhythm

You don't schedule discovery runs manually. Guhan runs any active source on the List at a rhythm that matches your **daily prospect target** — usually **once per day per signal**, spread across the day to look natural.

<Note>
  You can set a **daily prospect target** on the List (e.g., "give me 25 new prospects per day"). Once the target is hit, Guhan pauses discovery for the day and picks up tomorrow.
</Note>

## Deleting a List

Deleting a List:
- Does **not** delete the prospects themselves (they keep existing, just no longer in this List).
- Does **not** stop in-flight Campaign sequences (they finish per the sequence they started).
- **Does** retire the sources feeding this List.
