**A prospect is one real person in your workspace.** Open **Prospects** in the sidebar and you arrive on the **People** tab: every person across all your Lists, in one table. (The **Companies** tab sits next to it; see [Companies](/audience/companies).)

The table shows name, job title, company, location, email, phone, fit, status and whether they are enriched. Extra columns (headline, industry, company details, Lists, Campaigns, dates) can be switched on from the column menu.

- **Filter** with rules: job title, company, location, email, phone, LinkedIn URL, status, List, signal source and Campaign. Rules combine with "and".
- **Edit in place**: click a cell to edit name, title, company, location, email or phone. Editing a company detail such as industry or size changes it for the company record, and so for every prospect there.
- **Export CSV**: exports the filtered view, or only the rows you selected. Up to 10,000 prospects per export; narrow your filters if you have more.
- **Rejected people**: by default the table shows people who were added to your Lists. Add the **Include pipeline-rejected** filter to see people Guhan found but filtered out. See [How prospects get added](/audience/lists/how-prospects-get-added#why-someone-was-not-added).

### Status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Cold** | In your workspace but not in a sequence yet |
| **Active** | Part-way through a Campaign sequence |
| **Replied** | Has replied to a message |
| **Paused** | Their sequence is paused |
| **Do not contact** | On your [Do Not Contact](/workspace-settings/do-not-contact) list |
| **Unmailable** | A channel failed, for example a bounced email |
| **Rejected** | Found by a search but filtered out; only shown with the filter above |

## The prospect page

Click a name to open the prospect in a side panel, or expand it to a full page. Tabs:

- **Details**: editable fields, with an **Enrich** button that refreshes their LinkedIn profile.
- **Overview**: contact channels, company, **Signals: why we matched**, List memberships, and role and experience.
- **Research**: the research brief, fit rating and per-Product ratings. See [How prospects get added](/audience/lists/how-prospects-get-added#the-fit-rating).
- **Activity**: every message, reply and event for this person.

## Contact details and reveal

LinkedIn is the main channel. Email and phone are not included by default.

- **Reveal work email**: 4 credits.
- **Reveal phone number**: 12 credits. Phone is needed for WhatsApp.

You are only charged when a result is actually found. Reveal needs the prospect's LinkedIn URL. You can reveal one prospect from their page, or many at once; see [Enrichment](/audience/enrichment#reveal-email-and-phone).

## Removing a prospect

On a prospect's page (the **...** menu) or in a List's table (row menu, or select several), choose **Remove from outreach**.

- The prospect's status becomes **unsubscribed** and any active or paused Campaign sequence for them stops.
- The person is **not erased**. Their record stays in your workspace.
- This is not Do Not Contact. If you never want to reach this person or company again, add them to [Do Not Contact](/workspace-settings/do-not-contact).

Other actions in the **...** menu: **Pause enrollments** and **Flag for handoff**, which marks the conversation as warm so a person takes over.

<Note>
  To permanently delete a person's data (for example, a privacy request), contact support. Ask Guhan cannot delete records either.
</Note>

## If something looks off

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A prospect has no job title or company">
    They have probably not been enriched. Use the **Enrich** button on their page, or the sparkle on the column header for many at once. See [Enrichment](/audience/enrichment).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The fit rating says Pending…">
    The research step has not finished, or research brief generation is turned off in your enrichment options.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I cannot reveal an email">
    Reveal needs a LinkedIn URL on the prospect. If one is present and nothing comes back, you are not charged.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
