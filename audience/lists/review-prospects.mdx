---
title: Reviewing prospects
description: What to check after the first prospects are added to a List, and how to tighten your rules if Guhan is picking the wrong people.
---

**Check the first batch before you point a Campaign at the List.** A few minutes now is cheaper than messaging the wrong people.

## Check the List

Open the List from **Lists** in the sidebar.

1. **Prospects tab**: scan the table. Sort by **Fit score** to see the strongest matches first. Do the names, titles and companies look like who you meant?
2. **Sources tab**: look at the watch activity. It shows how many profiles were found, added and rejected. Click into a watch to see each profile under **Landed** (added to your List), **Rejected** and **In flight**.
3. **Rejected**: open a few. If Guhan rejected people you wanted, the reason tells you which rule is too strict. See [How prospects get added](/audience/lists/how-prospects-get-added#why-someone-was-not-added).

## Open a prospect

Click a row to open the prospect. The tabs are:

- **Details**: name, role, contact fields, company and location. Most fields can be edited in place.
- **Overview**: contact channels, company, **Signals: why we matched** (the post, hire or event that brought them in), List memberships, and role and experience.
- **Research**: the research brief, the fit rating and score, and a rating per Product if you have several.
- **Activity**: messages and replies, once a Campaign starts.

Ask: **would I send this person a personalised message right now?**

- **Yes**: keep them.
- **No**: take them out of outreach (see [Prospects](/audience/prospects#removing-a-prospect)), add them to [Do Not Contact](/workspace-settings/do-not-contact) if you never want to reach them, and tighten the source's rules so similar people do not come back.

## Tighten the rules

Open **Edit** on the List to change the source. Common fixes:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Wrong seniority">
    Your job titles may be missing the wording people actually use. "Sales Director" and "Director of Sales" are different strings. Use **Suggest variants** to add the forms you want, and set the **Seniority** rule.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Wrong industry">
    "Software Development" covers a lot. Pick a narrower industry, add a headcount range, or use a signal to narrow further.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Wrong location">
    A city rule only matches that city. Use the country if you meant the country.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Almost nobody is added">
    Every rule you fill in is required, so a long list of rules can leave very little. Remove the ones you do not strictly need. The reasons under **Rejected** show which rule is rejecting the most.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Most prospects have no email">
    Email and phone are not included by default. You can [reveal them](/audience/enrichment#reveal-email-and-phone) for 4 and 12 credits each, or turn on reveal in your enrichment options.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Mostly Moderate or Weak fit">
    The List is matching your rules but not what you sell. Rewrite the search around your real customer, or pair it with a signal that is closer to buying intent. Also check that your [Brand & Voice](/brand-and-voice/overview) and [Products](/brand-and-voice/products) describe who you sell to, because the fit rating is based on them.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Rule changes apply to future runs. They do not remove people already in the List.
