**A company record is the employer shared by your prospects.** If three prospects work at Acme, they all point to one Acme record. That is useful for account-based selling: open a company and see everyone you have found there.

## The Companies tab

Open **Prospects** in the sidebar and switch to the **Companies** tab. (Older links to the Companies page redirect here.)

- **Filter** by industry, country, city, headcount and status.
- **Edit in place**: name, industry, size, location and domain can be edited in the table. The change shows on every prospect at that company.
- **Create company**: add one by hand with name, website, LinkedIn URL, industry, location, employee range, founded year, description and notes. Most companies arrive on their own when a prospect is added.
- By default the table shows companies with at least one prospect in your Lists. If there are companies with none yet, a filter option lets you include them.

## Enrich a company

Companies often arrive with only a name. Enrichment fills in industry, headcount, HQ, founded year and description from the company's LinkedIn page.

- **One company**: open it and click **Enrich** on the Details tab.
- **Many**: select rows and click **Enrich company** in the bar at the bottom, or click the sparkle on the Industry, Size or Location column header to enrich the selection.

Each company costs 1 credit. It needs the company's LinkedIn URL. See [Enrichment](/audience/enrichment).

## The company page

Click a company to open it in a side panel. The expand icon at the top left (**Open full record**) opens the same record as a full page. Tabs:

- **Details**: editable company fields.
- **People**: every prospect you have found at this company.
- **Lists**: the Lists those prospects are in.
- **Research**: a company brief, plus recent funding and hiring highlights where available.
- **Activity**: messages and replies across the people there.

## Block a company

If you never want to contact anyone at a company, open it and choose **... → Block company**.

- The company's domain is added to your [Do Not Contact](/workspace-settings/do-not-contact) list. Future prospects at that domain are rejected, and in-flight sequences for matching prospects are paused.
- Common uses: your own company, competitors, and existing customers you do not want to cold-message.
- **Unblock** reverses the company's status only. The domain stays on your Do Not Contact list until you remove it there.

Blocking needs a domain on the company to take effect. Without one, only the status changes.

## Delete a company

**... → Delete** permanently removes the company record. Prospects there are kept, but lose their company link until they are enriched again. If you only want to stop outreach, block the company instead.

## If something looks off

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Company enrichment did nothing">
    The company needs a LinkedIn URL. Add one in the Details tab, then enrich again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Two records for the same company">
    Companies are matched by domain, and by name when there is no domain. Different spellings can create two records. Assign the prospect to the existing company from the company cell in the People table instead of typing a new name.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
