**Open the Inbox, start at "Needs you", and clear it from the top.**

Every row in "Needs you" has one primary action at the bottom of the thread. This page walks through each of them.

## The routine

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Inbox">
    Sidebar, Inbox. Leave the scope on **Mine** to see your own conversations. Use the channel tabs to narrow to Email, LinkedIn, or WhatsApp.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start with meeting requests">
    Rows tagged **Schedule** are prospects who asked to meet. Open one and click **Send link**. Guhan creates a booking link and sends it in the thread. See [Meetings](/meetings/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve drafts">
    Rows tagged **Approve** have a drafted reply waiting. Read it, then click **Approve & send**, or **Edit** to change it first, or **Reject** to discard it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take over the rest">
    Rows tagged **Take over** are yours to write. Click **Take over**, then reply in the composer at the bottom.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Acknowledge the FYIs">
    Rows tagged **FYI** need no action. Click **Got it** to clear them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What each button does

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Approve & send">
    Sends the drafted reply from the same sender account that started the conversation. To change the wording first, click **Edit**. The draft opens in the composer, and **Approve & send** sends your version.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Reject">
    Discards the draft. Nothing is sent.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Take over">
    Makes the conversation yours and stops every automated sequence running on that same thread and channel. This is deliberate: an automated message arriving in the middle of a human conversation reads as spam. Other channels for the same prospect keep running unless you take those over too.

    If you start typing in the composer on a conversation that is not yet assigned to you, the same thing happens.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Assign to">
    Next to **Take over**. Pick a teammate and the conversation moves to their Inbox, still waiting for them to act.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Send link">
    On a meeting request. Creates a booking link and sends it in the thread. Needs a connected calendar. See [Calendar integrations](/meetings/calendar-integrations).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Decide">
    When Guhan needs a choice from you, each option appears as a button. Hover an option to see why Guhan suggested it. Click one and Guhan carries on with your choice.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Got it">
    Acknowledges an FYI. No message is sent.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Reopen">
    On a closed thread. Reopens it so you can reply.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `j` / `k` | Move down or up the list |
| `/` | Jump to search |
| `a` | Open the approve action |
| `t` | Open take over |
| `Cmd` or `Ctrl` + `Enter` | Send from the composer |

## Who sees what

Conversations are tied to the sender account that sent the last message, so they show up for the person who owns that account. **Mine** shows your conversations. Anyone with permission to see every conversation can switch to **Team**.

## If something's off

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Approve & send fails">
    A message explains why. The most common cause is a sender account that needs reconnecting. Fix it under Settings, Sender accounts, then try again. See [Sender accounts](/get-started/sender-accounts/overview).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The composer tells me to take over first">
    The conversation is assigned to someone else, or Guhan's sequence is still running on it. Click **Take over** to reply.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Two drafts for the same prospect">
    If the prospect is in more than one campaign, each can draft a reply. When you send one, the other drafts for the same message are cleared automatically.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
