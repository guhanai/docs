**Guhan reads every reply, decides what it means, and acts on it. Whether it also writes a response is up to you.**

You set one workspace-wide rule, and you can override it at any step of a campaign. Meeting requests are never answered automatically, whatever you choose.

## What Guhan does with each reply

| What the prospect said | What Guhan does |
|---|---|
| **Interested** ("sounds good, tell me more") | Follows your reply policy: hand it to you, or draft a reply. |
| **Asked for a meeting** | Always hands it to a person, with a **Schedule** task so you can send a booking link. |
| **Asked a question** | Follows your reply policy. |
| **Said no** | Stops the sequence for that prospect. Nothing is sent. |
| **Asked to unsubscribe** | Stops the sequence for that prospect. Nothing is sent. |
| **Out of office** | Pauses the sequence for 7 days, then resumes. |
| **Not now** | Pauses the sequence for 30 days, resumes afterwards, and leaves you a note in the Inbox so you can follow up personally if you want. |

Guhan never auto-replies to a no, an unsubscribe, an out-of-office, or a not-now.

<Warning>
  Stopping a sequence on a no or an unsubscribe does not add the person to your Do Not Contact list. If you want them blocked across every campaign, add their email or domain yourself. See [Do Not Contact](/workspace-settings/do-not-contact).
</Warning>

<Note>
  When Guhan is not confident that a reply is a no or an unsubscribe, it does not stop the sequence. The thread comes to your Inbox as a **Take over** task so a person decides.
</Note>

## Reply policy

Settings, Sending, **Reply handling**. This is the default for any step that does not have its own AI reply step on the campaign.

Auto-send is limited to a single reply per conversation under the workspace policy. If the prospect replies again, or if a send fails, the thread comes to you as a **Take over** task.

## Per-step control: the AI reply step

On the campaign canvas, an **AI reply** step placed after a wait-for-reply step overrides the workspace policy for that point in the sequence. Explicit steps always win over the workspace default.

It has three settings:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Send for approval** | On: the draft waits in your Inbox for approval. Off: it sends automatically. |
| **Max consecutive replies** | How many replies Guhan can send in a row before the sequence moves on (1 to 10). 1 means a single reply. |
| **Reply timeout** | How long to wait for the prospect to answer between replies (1 hour to 30 days). |

The step has four exits you wire up on the canvas: **Conversation done**, **Meeting requested**, **Not interested**, and **Reply timeout**. Out-of-office and not-now replies pause and resume on their own, and failed sends go to the Inbox for a manual retry.

For building the canvas, see [Build a campaign](/campaigns/build-a-campaign).

## How drafts are written

A draft uses the full conversation so far, your [Brand & Voice](/brand-and-voice/overview), the product the campaign is selling, and what Guhan knows about the prospect. If the product has objection answers and offers set up, the draft can use them. See [Products](/brand-and-voice/products).

When a draft is waiting, you will see it in the thread with a marker showing it was written by Guhan. Approve it as is, edit it, or reject it. See [Handle replies](/inbox/handle-replies).

## Cost

An AI-drafted reply costs 1 credit. Replies you write yourself cost nothing. When a campaign step is set to approval, the credit is charged when you approve and send. See [Plans and credits](/workspace-settings/plans-and-credits) for every credit cost in one place.

## If something's off

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A reply I expected Guhan to answer came to me instead">
    Meeting requests always come to a person. Beyond that, check Settings, Sending, Reply handling, and check whether the campaign has its own AI reply step that overrides it. Auto-send also falls back to you after one reply, on a send failure, or when credits run out.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Guhan stopped a sequence and it should not have">
    Reply meaning is read by a model and can be wrong on short or ambiguous messages. Guhan holds back from stopping when it is unsure, but it can still misjudge a clear-sounding reply. Open the conversation in the Inbox and reply yourself.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
