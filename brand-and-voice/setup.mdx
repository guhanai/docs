**Work through the tabs in order. Each tile saves on its own, and changes apply to messages drafted from then on.**

## Brand

Click **Edit** on a tile to change it.

- **Industry**: pick from the list, or search. This is the industry you operate in.
- **Company description & value proposition**: two or three plain sentences on what you do and why it matters.
- **Customer pain points**: the specific problems your customers have.

<Tip>
  Specific beats general. "Sales teams spend two to three hours a day on manual prospecting and the lists go stale before anyone works them" gives Guhan far more to work with than "prospecting is hard".
</Tip>

## Voice

- **Tone**: one of Professional, Conversational, Direct, Authoritative, Technical, Casual, or Consultative. Each has a short description in the picker. Conversational is the default.
- **Preferred language**: the language Guhan should write in. Treat it as guidance rather than a guarantee.
- **Words Guhan won't write**: phrases or words to keep out of every message, such as "just wanted to circle back". Type each one and press Enter or a comma. Guhan strictly keeps these out of messages it drafts.

<Note>
  There is no "always include" list. Forcing a phrase into every message made messages read as repetitive, so your positioning belongs in the company description and on your products instead.
</Note>

## Competitors

Add each competitor with:

- **Name**
- **Differentiation**: how you differ when this competitor comes up, for example where you win and where you would concede.

Guhan uses the differentiation when a prospect mentions that competitor. Use **New competitor** to add one, and the search box to find an existing one.

## Templates and Products & Services

These two tabs are covered on their own pages: [Message templates](/brand-and-voice/templates) and [Products](/brand-and-voice/products).

## Keeping it stable

Changing tone or the never-say list part-way through a campaign makes later messages sound different from earlier ones, which prospects notice. Settle it early and change it deliberately.

## If something's off

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Messages still use a phrase I banned">
    The ban applies to messages Guhan drafts. Check whether that step is set to **Fixed message**, where the text is sent as typed, and whether the phrase is in a template or the step's own instructions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My edits did not seem to apply">
    Messages drafted before you saved are not changed. Only newly drafted messages pick up the change.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Messages are generic">
    Add detail to the company description and pain points, then fill in the product the campaign is using. See [Products](/brand-and-voice/products).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
