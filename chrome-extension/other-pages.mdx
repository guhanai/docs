---
title: Company, event and network pages
description: Import a company's employees or an event's attendees into a Guhan List, and where to find your own followers and following.
---

The same checkboxes, bar and bulk import work wherever LinkedIn lists people. On a company or an event, the drawer first opens the list of people for you, and you import from it exactly as you would from a [search](/chrome-extension/searches-and-imports).

## A company's employees

On a company page, click **Open employees** in the drawer. LinkedIn's list of people who work there opens, with the Guhan checkboxes and bar on it, and **Bulk import…** works on it.

## An event's attendees

On an event page, click **Open attendees**. You don't need to be registered for the event. People who hide that they're attending won't appear.

<Frame caption="On an event page, Open attendees takes you to the people attending">
  <img src="/images/ext-event-attendees.webp" alt="The drawer on an event page with an Open attendees button" width="400" height="325" />
</Frame>

## Your connections, followers and following

These are your own lists, so there is nothing to open first. Go to the list on LinkedIn and the checkboxes and bar appear. See [Your connections, followers and following](/chrome-extension/searches-and-imports#your-connections-followers-and-following).
