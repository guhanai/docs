---
title: Company, event and network pages
description: Import a company's employees, an event's attendees, or your own followers and following into a Guhan List.
---

The same import works wherever LinkedIn lists people. In each case the panel opens that list of people, and you import from it exactly as you would from a [search](/chrome-extension/searches-and-imports).

## A company's employees

On a company page, click **Open employees**. LinkedIn's list of people who work there opens, and the panel's **Import…** works on it.

<Frame caption="On a company page, Open employees takes you to its people">
  <img src="/images/ext-company-employees.webp" alt="The panel on a company page with an Open employees button" width="400" height="380" />
</Frame>

## An event's attendees

On an event page, click **Open attendees**. You don't need to be registered for the event. People who hide that they're attending won't appear.

<Frame caption="On an event page, Open attendees takes you to the people attending">
  <img src="/images/ext-event-attendees.webp" alt="The panel on an event page with an Open attendees button" width="400" height="430" />
</Frame>

## Your followers and following

On your own followers or following list, choose how many people to import. Guhan scrolls the list for you, a few seconds at a time. LinkedIn doesn't show anyone else's followers.

<Frame caption="Import the first people on your followers or following list">
  <img src="/images/ext-followers.webp" alt="The panel on the followers list with an Import your followers card" width="400" height="720" />
</Frame>

<Note>
  People from your followers and following lists are matched by their LinkedIn member ID. Someone who's already in Guhan under only a profile link may be added again.
</Note>
