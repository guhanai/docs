## Install and connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Guhan to Chrome">
    Install the extension and pin it to your toolbar so the icon is always one click away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open LinkedIn">
    Go to a profile, a people search, a company page or an event.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Guhan icon">
    The panel opens beside the page and shows what it found.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your account">
    Click **Connect to Guhan** and sign in. You only do this once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Around the panel

- **Workspace.** If you belong to more than one workspace, switch between them from the name at the top of the panel.
- **Credits.** Your credit balance sits next to the workspace name. It updates after anything that spends credits.
- **The ⋯ menu.** **Open Guhan** takes you to your workspace, **How to use** opens a short illustrated guide, and **Sign out** disconnects the extension.

## Which pages it works on

| On this LinkedIn page | The panel |
|---|---|
| A profile | Shows where the person stands in Guhan and lets you [add them](/chrome-extension/profiles). |
| A people search | Shows everyone on the page and lets you [import or pick people](/chrome-extension/searches-and-imports). |
| A company page | Offers **Open employees**. See [other pages](/chrome-extension/other-pages). |
| An event page | Offers **Open attendees**. See [other pages](/chrome-extension/other-pages). |
| Your followers or following list | Lets you [import](/chrome-extension/other-pages#your-followers-and-following) the first people on it. |
| Anything else (your feed, messages) | Asks you to open one of the pages above. Other pages aren't read. |
