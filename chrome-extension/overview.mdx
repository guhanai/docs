---
title: Guhan Chrome extension
description: Add people to Guhan from LinkedIn without leaving the page. Tick people on LinkedIn, see who's already in Guhan, add them to a List, import a whole search, and enrich them after adding.
---

**The Guhan Chrome extension works inside LinkedIn.** A small Guhan icon sits on every LinkedIn page. Click it and the Guhan drawer opens on the right of the same window. Tick people in a search or in your network, or open a profile, and the drawer shows who is already in your workspace and adds people to a List in a click. What happens next (messages, follow-ups, replies) runs from Guhan, as usual.

## What you can do

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Pick people where you find them" icon="square-check" href="/chrome-extension/searches-and-imports#pick-people-on-the-page">

  <Card title="Enrich after adding" icon="sparkles" href="/chrome-extension/profiles#enrich-them-after-adding">

## Read-only on LinkedIn

The extension never sends a message, an invite, a comment or a follow on LinkedIn. Everything you send goes out from Guhan, through your [sender accounts](/get-started/sender-accounts/overview) and their [sending limits](/campaigns/sending-safety). See [Safety and troubleshooting](/chrome-extension/safety-and-troubleshooting).
