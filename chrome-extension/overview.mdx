---
title: Guhan Chrome extension
description: Add people to Guhan from LinkedIn without leaving the page. See who's already in Guhan, add people to a List, import a search, and enrich them after adding.
---

**The Guhan Chrome extension puts Guhan beside LinkedIn.** Open a profile, a people search, a company page or an event, and a panel shows who is already in your workspace and lets you add people to a List in a click. What happens next (messages, follow-ups, replies) runs from Guhan, as usual.

## What you can do

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="See who's already in Guhan" icon="user-check" href="/chrome-extension/profiles#see-whos-already-in-guhan">
    Every profile and search result shows whether the person is new, on a List, in a live Campaign, has replied, or is marked Do Not Contact.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Add people to a List" icon="plus" href="/chrome-extension/profiles#add-them-to-a-list">
    One person from their profile, or many from a search. Active Campaigns pick them up automatically.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Import from a search" icon="list-checks" href="/chrome-extension/searches-and-imports">
    Choose how many people to add, up to 999, and Guhan goes through the result pages for you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Read the full profile" icon="scroll" href="/chrome-extension/profiles#read-the-full-profile">
    Load a profile's Experience and Education so the job title and company come with the person.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Enrich after adding" icon="sparkles" href="/chrome-extension/profiles#enrich-them-after-adding">
    Optionally add work email, phone, a research brief and more. The exact cost is shown first.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Company, event and network pages" icon="building" href="/chrome-extension/other-pages">
    Import a company's employees, an event's attendees, or your own followers and following.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Read-only on LinkedIn

The extension only reads. It never sends a message, an invite, a comment or a follow on LinkedIn. Everything you send goes out from Guhan, through your [sender accounts](/get-started/sender-accounts/overview) and their [sending limits](/campaigns/sending-safety). See [Safety and troubleshooting](/chrome-extension/safety-and-troubleshooting).
