**Ask Guhan is your direct line to Guhan, your AI SDR. Sidebar → `+ Ask Guhan`, or WhatsApp Guhan from your phone. Ask questions, assign new work, request diagnostics — the same channel you would use to message a human SDR on your team.**

The mental model: your workspace has an AI SDR named Guhan. You can message it inside the app or over WhatsApp. Same agent, same memory, same tool access on both surfaces.

Guhan can do almost anything you can do in the app — set up a new Campaign, spin up a List with signals, pull a report, explain why a prospect got filtered. Just type what you want.

<Note>
  Ask Guhan is in beta. Guhan can do most things you can do in the UI, but occasionally makes mistakes. Verify anything you'd act on before hitting Send.
</Note>

## Things Guhan won't do

By design:

- **Delete records** — Guhan can create, update, pause, and unbind — but never delete. If you need to delete a prospect / list / Campaign, use the UI's kebab menu. This is a safety line, not a limitation of the agent.
- **Change other users' data** — Guhan's actions only touch your workspace and its records.
- **Complete OAuth flows** — if you say "connect my LinkedIn", Guhan points you at the Settings page where the OAuth handshake happens. Guhan won't do that step for you.

## How to open the chat

- Click **`+ Ask Guhan`** in the sidebar
- Or press `Cmd+K` from anywhere in the app (opens a mini search + chat)

## Threads and history

Every conversation with Guhan is a **thread** — a chat you can come back to later. Guhan remembers what you discussed in that thread.

- **New thread** — every fresh `+ Ask Guhan` click starts one
- **History** — the sidebar shows your recent threads; click any to reopen
- Full history page at **History** in the sidebar

## Tool calls in the chat

When Guhan does something on your behalf, you'll see a **tool card** in the message stream. Example:

> **Tool: List prospects** — filter: List = "Enterprise VPs"
>
> Found 47 prospects.
> [table shown]

Every tool call is scoped to your workspace (Guhan can never see other workspaces' data).

## The 👍 / 👎 feedback

Every response has a thumb-up / thumb-down. Click them — the feedback goes to us and improves how Guhan responds next time.

## Guhan on WhatsApp

If you connect a WhatsApp sender and turn on **Ask Guhan on WhatsApp** on that account's row (Settings → Sender accounts), you can text Guhan from your phone by messaging your own WhatsApp number. Guhan replies in that self-thread — same agent, same memory, same tool access. See [Connect WhatsApp](/get-started/sender-accounts/connect-whatsapp) for setup.

## Credits

Talking to Guhan is free within your daily token allowance (50k tokens per user per day on Starter). Beyond that, overflow tokens burn from your workspace credit balance at 1 credit per 50k tokens.
