Guhan is an **AI SDR** — a Sales Development Representative that runs the full outbound workflow on your sales team.

Everything Guhan does is what a good SDR does: understand your business, find prospects who fit your ideal customer, research each one, draft hyper-personalized outreach across LinkedIn / Email / WhatsApp, handle replies, and book meetings for the sales team.

## Message quality follows business context

The quality of messages Guhan writes is a direct function of the business context you give it.

Rich context — your industry, your customer pain points, your products and their value propositions with case studies, your brand voice, how you position against competitors — produces messages that read as if someone who understands the customer's problem wrote them. Thin context produces generic peer register and generic pain framing.

Every setup step in Guhan is a context-gathering step. The 10-minute quickstart is not tool configuration; it is briefing an SDR on your business.

## Who Guhan works for

Founders and sales teams selling to a defined buyer persona.

Guhan can run your outbound if you can answer these three questions:

1. **Who is your ideal customer?** ("VP of Sales at SaaS companies in the US")
2. **What signals indicate they are ready to buy?** ("Raised a Series A" or "Posted about hiring SDRs")
3. **What is your pitch?** ("We help X do Y — here is the value proposition")

To onboard Guhan you need only your **website URL**. Guhan reads your site and drafts everything else; you review and refine.
