Guhan reads your site, learns what you sell and who buys it, then goes and finds real people showing buying signals right now — and starts a personal conversation with each one on the channels you choose.

You show up when there's a real reply.

## Who Guhan is

Guhan is an **AI sales agent** — not a tool you operate, an agent you brief.

The relationship: you're the founder or head of sales. Guhan is the SDR working alongside you. You give Guhan the context — what you sell, who buys it, how you sound, what "ready to buy" looks like. Guhan handles the rest: finding people, researching each one, writing the outreach, running the follow-up, reading replies, escalating what actually needs your judgment.

**You brief. Guhan runs. You close.**

## Who Guhan works for

Founders and small sales teams who sell to a specific kind of buyer.

If you can answer these three questions, Guhan can run your outbound:

1. **Who's your ideal customer?** ("VP of Sales at SaaS companies in the US")
2. **What tells you they're ready?** ("They raised a Series A" or "They posted about hiring SDRs")
3. **What's your pitch?** ("We help X do Y — here's the value")

To hire Guhan, all you need is your **website URL**. Guhan reads your site and drafts everything else — you review + refine.
