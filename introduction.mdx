Guhan is an **AI SDR**: a Sales Development Representative that runs the outbound workflow for your sales team. You brief it on your business, point it at the people you want to reach, and it does the repetitive work — finding prospects, researching each one, writing the messages, following up, and sorting the replies. You step in where judgment matters.

## The workflow in one line

**Set up Brand & Voice → import leads → run a Campaign → handle replies → book meetings.**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up Brand & Voice">
    Give it your brand voice and your products. Everything Guhan writes is shaped by this context, so the more specific you are, the more your messages sound like someone who understands the buyer's problem.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import leads">
    On the Prospects page, click Import leads. Find people with search criteria or a buying signal, upload a CSV, or add someone by hand. They land in a List.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run a Campaign">
    A Campaign takes one or more Lists and runs a sequence of steps across LinkedIn, Email, and WhatsApp. Guhan drafts each message for each person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Handle replies">
    Replies land in your Inbox. Guhan classifies them, and depending on your reply rules either escalates, drafts a response for approval, or answers automatically. Meeting requests always go to a person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book meetings">
    Connect a calendar and Guhan shares your booking link. Booked meetings show up under Meetings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who it's for

Founders and sales teams selling to a defined buyer. You can get started if you can answer three questions:

1. **Who is your ideal customer?** For example, "VP of Sales at SaaS companies in the US."
2. **What tells you they might be ready to buy?** For example, "raised funding" or "posted about hiring SDRs."
3. **What do you offer them?** A short statement of the problem you solve.

To onboard, you only need your **website URL**. Guhan reads your site and drafts the rest for you to review.

## Your workspace at a glance

The sidebar follows the workflow: **Home**, **Inbox**, **Meetings**, **Campaigns**, **Brand & Voice**, **Lists**, **Prospects**, and **Workspace settings**. Home is your daily snapshot; the rest is where the work happens.
