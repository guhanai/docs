By the end of this page you'll have a List collecting prospects and a Campaign with a starter sequence, ready to launch on the channel you connect (LinkedIn, Email, or WhatsApp).

<Note>
  Every onboarding step is Guhan collecting business context from you, the way you'd brief a new SDR. The quality of the messages Guhan writes later follows the quality of what you give it here.
</Note>

## Before you start

- **Your company website URL.** Guhan reads it to draft your brand summary.
- **A sender account to connect after launch** — a LinkedIn account, a Gmail or Outlook address, or WhatsApp. You pick how Guhan reaches prospects on the launch screen, then connect the account from Settings, Sender accounts.
- **A payment method for the trial.** Signup asks you to authorize a card or UPI mandate. Authorizing moves no money; the first charge happens when the 7-day trial ends, and you can cancel before then.

## 1. Sign up

<Note>
  Sign-up comes first. The setup wizard that follows shows five numbered steps, which are sections 2 to 6 on this page.
</Note>

Go to [guhan.ai](https://guhan.ai) and choose **Get started**. Enter your email, confirm the 6-digit code we send you, then set your name and password. Next you'll authorize the trial mandate, then start onboarding.

Your trial starts with 1,000 credits. When your subscription starts, your balance resets to 2,000 credits for each license. See [Plans and credits](/workspace-settings/plans-and-credits).

## 2. Paste your website

The first onboarding screen asks for your company website. Paste your main marketing site and click **Analyze**. Guhan reads a handful of pages from your site and pre-fills the rest of the wizard.

## 3. Confirm your brand

Guhan drafts your company name, industry, what you sell, and the pain you solve, and marks the draft as AI-generated. Read every line and fix anything that doesn't sound like you. Every future message builds on this summary.

<Tip>
  You can edit all of this later under **Brand & Voice**. Nothing is locked.
</Tip>

<Frame caption="Confirm your brand: Guhan drafts it and marks what it wrote as AI-generated. Edit anything that is off">

## 4. Say who to reach and which companies

- **Who to reach** — job titles and locations. Type a title like "VP of Sales", then click **Suggest variants** to get close alternatives you can keep or remove.
- **Which companies** — industries and company sizes. You can also turn on **buying signals** here. Each signal you pick gets its own List.

## 5. Review five real prospects

Guhan runs a real search and shows five people who match. Click **Reject** on anyone you would not message, then click **Looks good — continue**. Rejecting pulls fresh candidates, up to three times. The people you keep seed your first List.

## 6. Name your Campaign and launch

The last screen summarizes your brand, audience, and signals, and asks you to name your Campaign. Click **Looks good — Launch agent**, then pick how Guhan should reach prospects: **Multichannel** (LinkedIn invite and message, with email follow-ups), **LinkedIn only** or **Email only**. Depending on your plan, some modes show **Upgrade to unlock**. You connect the actual accounts after launch, from Settings, Sender accounts.

Launch creates:

- **One List per signal you picked**, or a single List if you picked none.
- **One Campaign** that uses all of those Lists.
- **A starter sequence** that matches the mode you picked: LinkedIn steps, email steps, or both.

You arrive on the Campaign's **Leads** tab while Guhan runs its first search.

<Warning>
  Your Campaign is created as a **draft**. Nothing is sent until you review the sequence, connect a sender, and launch the Campaign. The setup screen says the campaign stays paused until at least one sender is connected. See [Launch and manage](/campaigns/launch-and-manage).
</Warning>

## If something's off

- **No prospects yet.** The first search takes a little while. Check the Leads tab; if it stays empty, loosen your titles or industries on the List. See [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting).
- **Sender not connected.** Connect one under **Settings → Sender accounts**. See [Sender accounts](/get-started/sender-accounts/overview).
