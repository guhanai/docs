By the end of this page you'll have a List collecting prospects and a Campaign with a starter sequence, ready to launch on the channel you connect (LinkedIn, Email, or WhatsApp).

<Note>
  Every onboarding step is Guhan collecting business context from you, the way you'd brief a new SDR. The quality of the messages Guhan writes later follows the quality of what you give it here.
</Note>

## Before you start

- **Your company website URL.** Guhan reads it to draft your brand summary.
- **A channel to send from** — a LinkedIn account, a Gmail or Outlook address, or WhatsApp. You pick one on the launch screen; you can add more later.
- **A payment method for the trial.** Signup asks you to authorize a card or UPI mandate. Authorizing moves no money; the first charge happens when the 7-day trial ends, and you can cancel before then.

## 1. Sign up

Go to [guhan.ai](https://guhan.ai) and choose **Get started**. Enter your email, confirm the 6-digit code we send you, then set your name and password. Next you'll authorize the trial mandate, then start onboarding.

Your trial starts with 1,000 credits. When your subscription starts, your balance resets to 2,000 credits for each license. See [Plans and credits](/workspace-settings/plans-and-credits).

## 2. Paste your website

The first onboarding screen asks for your company website. Paste your main marketing site and continue. Guhan reads a handful of pages from your site and pre-fills the rest of the wizard.

## 3. Confirm your brand

Guhan drafts your company name, industry, what you sell, and the pain you solve, and marks each drafted field as AI-generated. Read every line and fix anything that doesn't sound like you. Every future message builds on this summary.

<Tip>
  You can edit all of this later under **Brand & Voice**. Nothing is locked.
</Tip>

## 4. Say who to reach and which companies

- **Who to reach** — job titles and locations. Type a title like "VP of Sales" and Guhan suggests close variations you can keep or remove.
- **Which companies** — industries and company sizes. You can also turn on **buying signals** here. Each signal you pick gets its own List.

## 5. Review five real prospects

Guhan runs a real search and shows five people who match. Accept the ones you'd actually message and reject the rest. Rejecting pulls fresh candidates, up to three times. Accepted prospects seed your first List.

## 6. Name your Campaign and launch

The last screen summarizes your brand, audience, and signals, and asks you to name your Campaign. When you launch, Guhan asks which channel you want to send from. Pick one and connect it there, or skip and connect later.

Launch creates:

- **One List per signal you picked**, or a single List if you picked none.
- **One Campaign** that uses all of those Lists.
- **A starter sequence** that matches the channel you connected. LinkedIn gets a profile view, an invite, and up to two DMs. Email gets two emails. WhatsApp gets two messages. If you connected nothing, you start with a blank canvas.

You arrive on the Campaign's **Leads** tab while Guhan runs its first search.

<Warning>
  Your Campaign is created as a **draft**. Nothing is sent until you review the sequence, connect a sender if you haven't, and launch the Campaign. See [Launch and manage](/campaigns/launch-and-manage).
</Warning>

## If something's off

- **No prospects yet.** The first search takes a little while. Check the Leads tab; if it stays empty, loosen your titles or industries on the List. See [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting).
- **Sender not connected.** Connect one under **Settings → Sender accounts**. See [Sender accounts](/get-started/sender-accounts/overview).
