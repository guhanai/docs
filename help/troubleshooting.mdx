## Sender accounts

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A sender says it needs reauthorization">
    The connection expired, usually after a password change or a sign-out from all sessions.

    **Fix:** open **Settings → Sender accounts**, find the account, and reconnect it. Guhan shows a banner while any sender is broken, and sends from it are paused until it's fixed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A LinkedIn account was restricted">
    LinkedIn flagged activity and limited the account.

    **Fix:**
    1. Sign in to LinkedIn directly and follow what it asks.
    2. Once the restriction is lifted, reconnect the account in Guhan.
    3. Review your caps and sending window. See [Sending safety](/campaigns/sending-safety).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Emails are bouncing">
    A few bounces are normal. Many bounces usually mean a reputation or setup problem.

    **Check:**
    - Your domain has SPF, DKIM, and DMARC set up.
    - You're not sending far more than the mailbox is used to.
    - Your messages avoid spammy phrasing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="WhatsApp disconnected">
    The phone-linked session ended.

    **Fix:** reconnect the account under **Settings → Sender accounts** and scan the QR code with your phone.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't connect another sender">
    Each license includes one sender. Add a license or an extra-sender add-on under **Settings → Billing**. See [Plans and credits](/workspace-settings/plans-and-credits).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Lists and prospects

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A List isn't finding anyone">
    The filters may be too strict, or the List may be out of credits.

    **Check:**
    - **Credits.** Searches stop when the balance reaches zero.
    - **Titles.** Try broader titles.
    - **Company filters.** Loosen industry, size, or headquarters.
    - **Signal.** Remove or loosen the signal to see whether it's the cause.

    **To isolate it:** pause every signal source but one and see whether prospects get added.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A List is finding too many">
    Tighten the filters on the source: narrower titles, a smaller size range, or a stricter signal. Remember that every profile a watch returns costs a credit, even if it doesn't pass your filters.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The wrong seniority is getting added">
    Title matching is literal. "Sales Director" and "Director of Sales" match differently, so add the exact forms you want. Remove suggested variations that are below your target level.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Someone shows up as already in the workspace">
    Guhan skips people it already has, so nobody is contacted twice. Search the **Prospects** page for the name to find them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't add more prospects from a CSV">
    Uploads are limited by an active-prospect cap that scales with your licenses and senders. Remove prospects you no longer need or add a license. See [Plans and credits](/workspace-settings/plans-and-credits).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Campaigns

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My Campaign isn't sending">
    **Check:**
    - **It's still a draft.** Campaigns need to be launched.
    - **No sequence,** or the sequence has errors.
    - **No List** is selected as the audience.
    - **No sender** is assigned for a channel the sequence uses, or a sender is disconnected.
    - **Outside the sending window.** Sends wait for your active hours.
    - **Out of credits.** AI-drafted messages cost a credit each.
    - **The workspace is read-only.** See Billing below.

    An empty audience only warns; it doesn't block a launch. See [Launch and manage](/campaigns/launch-and-manage).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't edit my Campaign">
    A running Campaign has to be paused before you edit it. Pause it, make your changes, then resume. See [Launch and manage](/campaigns/launch-and-manage).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Nobody is replying">
    Read a few sent messages from a prospect's timeline. Do they sound like you? Tone in Brand & Voice, a heavy first-message pitch, and a weak signal fit are the usual causes. Try a softer opening and a tighter List.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Messages feel repetitive">
    Over-specific step briefs and too many required phrases produce samey drafts. Simplify the brief, trim your "never include" list to what matters, and turn on recent-post fetching for LinkedIn DM steps so messages reference real content.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Inbox and replies

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Replies aren't appearing">
    Check that the sender account is connected and healthy under **Settings → Sender accounts**, and reconnect it if it shows a problem. If a sender looks fine and replies are still missing, contact support.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Guhan didn't draft a reply">
    Drafting depends on your reply rules under **Settings → Sending & Reply Rules**. With **Escalate to a user**, replies come to you instead. Meeting requests always go to a person. AI drafts also need credits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A reply was classified wrongly">
    Take over the conversation and handle it yourself. Guhan labels replies as positive, meeting request, question, negative, unsubscribe, out of office, or not now. Low-confidence "negative" and "unsubscribe" verdicts are sent to a person rather than stopping the sequence.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Meetings

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Prospects can't find a time to book">
    Your calendar may show you as busy across the offered window. Check working hours, all-day blocking events, and that your meeting duration plus buffer fits your free gaps.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A meeting isn't on my calendar">
    Syncing can lag a little. If it persists, check the account's status under **Settings → Calendars** and reconnect it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A Cal.com or Calendly booking isn't tracked">
    Tracking relies on information carried in the booking link. Some link shorteners strip it. Use the link Guhan generates as is, and check the calendar's status under **Settings → Calendars**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My balance is 0, or sends stopped">
    Check **Settings → Billing**. Zero credits stops searches, enrichment, and AI drafting. A workspace is read-only if the trial ended without a subscription or a failed renewal wasn't fixed within 7 days; update your payment method to lift it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A plan change failed">
    This is usually the payment method. Update it under Billing and try again. Reducing licenses is blocked if you have more members or senders than the new count.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I was charged twice">
    Email [hello@guhan.ai](mailto:hello@guhan.ai) with the invoice details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
