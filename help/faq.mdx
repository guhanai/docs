## Getting started

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need Sales Navigator?">
    No. Prospect discovery doesn't use your LinkedIn account, so a regular LinkedIn account is enough for sending.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial, and do I need a card?">
    There's a 7-day trial. At signup you authorize a card or UPI mandate. Authorizing moves no money, and your first charge happens after the trial. See [Plans and credits](/workspace-settings/plans-and-credits).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use a personal Gmail or Outlook account?">
    Yes, you can connect Gmail and Outlook accounts as senders. See [Connect email](/get-started/sender-accounts/connect-email).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why isn't my Campaign sending?">
    Campaigns start as drafts. Nothing sends until you launch the Campaign, it has a sequence, and it has a connected sender. See [Launch and manage](/campaigns/launch-and-manage).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I import my own prospects?">
    Yes. Upload a CSV from **Import leads** on the Prospects page (or **Import → Import CSV** inside a List) and map your columns. You can choose to enrich the profiles after upload, which costs credits per profile. Uploads are limited by your active-prospect cap. See [Import leads](/audience/import-leads).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Sending and account safety

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will my LinkedIn account get restricted?">
    Guhan sends within daily caps on each sender account and spaces messages out, which keeps activity in a normal range. Raising caps or narrowing your sending window increases risk. See [Sending safety](/campaigns/sending-safety).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use my main LinkedIn account?">
    Yes. Sends come from the account you connect, under your name.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if my account gets restricted or disconnected?">
    Guhan pauses sends from that account and shows a banner. Resolve it with the platform if needed, then reconnect the account under **Settings → Sender accounts**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many senders can I connect?">
    One per license, on any channel, plus any extra-sender add-ons. See [Plans and credits](/workspace-settings/plans-and-credits).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Messages and personalization

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Guhan use my brand voice?">
    Yes. AI-drafted messages use your Brand & Voice tone and your "never include" list, along with your product and the prospect's profile. See [Brand & Voice](/brand-and-voice/overview).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I write the exact message myself?">
    Yes. A step can use a **Fixed message**, which sends your text with variables like the prospect's name filled in. Fixed messages aren't AI-drafted, so they don't cost an AI-message credit. A step can also be **Guided**, where you steer the draft, or **Auto-draft**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I review messages before they send?">
    Yes. On a step, turn on **Require approval before sending**. The draft lands in your Inbox tasks and nothing sends until you approve it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Guhan write in another language?">
    Set **Preferred language** in Brand & Voice. Options include several English variants, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese (Brazil), Dutch, Italian, and Japanese.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Prospects and discovery

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do prospects come from?">
    Guhan's managed data sources. It never searches from your personal LinkedIn account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do rejected prospects cost credits?">
    Yes. Discovery is charged for each profile a sweep returns, before Guhan checks fit, so a prospect that doesn't match your filters still costs one credit. Tighter filters on the source mean fewer wasted profiles. See [Plans and credits](/workspace-settings/plans-and-credits).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get work emails or phone numbers?">
    Guhan reveals them when a step needs them, or you can turn them on in enrichment settings. A work email costs 4 credits and a phone number 12, charged only when one is found.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I block people or companies?">
    Yes. Block email addresses and domains under **Settings → Do-Not-Contact list**, or use **Block** on a company. See [Do not contact](/workspace-settings/do-not-contact).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Guhan found far more prospects than I can handle">
    Tighten the filters on the List's signal sources, such as narrower titles, a smaller company size range, or a stricter signal. You can also pause a source.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Billing and credits

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What costs credits?">
    Finding, enriching, and writing cost credits. Sending is free. The full table is on [Plans and credits](/workspace-settings/plans-and-credits).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I run out?">
    Actions that spend credits are refused until you buy a pack or your next cycle starts. See [Plans and credits](/workspace-settings/plans-and-credits).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do credits roll over?">
    Monthly credits don't. Credits from packs last 12 months and are spent after your monthly credits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I cancel?">
    Admins can cancel from the Subscription tab on **Settings → Billing**. You keep access until the end of the trial or paid period.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get a refund or billing help?">
    Email [hello@guhan.ai](mailto:hello@guhan.ai) with your workspace name and the invoice in question.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Teams

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I invite teammates?">
    Yes, up to one member per license. Invite from **Settings → Team** and pick a role. See [Roles and permissions](/workspace-settings/roles-and-permissions).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can teammates share a sender account?">
    Each sender has one owner. Admins can reassign ownership.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I have separate workspaces?">
    Yes. An org admin can enable multiple workspaces under **Settings → Workspace & advanced**. Each workspace has its own senders, prospects, and billing.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Meetings

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Guhan work with Cal.com and Calendly?">
    Yes. Add your booking link under **Settings → Calendars**. See [Calendar integrations](/meetings/calendar-integrations).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Guhan book a meeting without me?">
    Meeting requests always go to a person. The prospect then picks a time from your booking link.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
