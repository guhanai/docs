title: Do not contact
description: Block email addresses and whole domains so Guhan never messages them, and see what happens to people already in flight.
---

The **Do-Not-Contact (DNC)** list is your workspace's block list. Anyone who matches an entry is never added to a List, and anyone already in a sequence is stopped.

## What you can block

| Entry | Example | Blocks |
|---|---|---|
| **Email address** | `bo@example.com` | That exact address |
| **Domain** | `acme.com` | Any email address at that domain |

Those are the only two kinds. There's no block for a LinkedIn profile URL or a phone number, so to keep someone out you block their email address or their company's domain.

<Tip>
  Add your own company's domain so Guhan never reaches out to your own team.
</Tip>

## Add entries

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the list">
    Go to **Settings → Do-Not-Contact list**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste entries">
    Put one email address or domain on each line. Entries are lowercased and trimmed, and adding one that already exists is harmless. Lines that aren't a valid email or domain are rejected and listed so you can fix them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to list">
    Choose **Add to list**. You can search existing entries from the same page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Block a company

On the **Companies** page or a company's detail page, choose **Block**. This adds the company's domain to the DNC list and marks the company as blocked, together in one step.

Unblocking a company doesn't remove its domain from the DNC list. Remove the entry on the DNC page if you want it gone.

## What happens when you add an entry

Adding an entry takes effect right away, including on people already in your workspace:

- **Prospects already in sequences** that match are paused and won't receive further messages.
- **Matching prospects in your Lists** are marked as unsubscribed.
- **New prospects** that match are rejected before they are added to a List.

## Removing an entry

Removing an entry doesn't bring anyone back. Prospects who were paused stay paused, and prospects marked as unsubscribed stay that way. To re-engage someone, add them back to a List and enroll them in a Campaign yourself.

## Good to know

- DNC is per workspace. Blocking `acme.com` in one workspace doesn't block it in another.
- You can add up to 500 entries at a time.
- There's no import or export; entries are added by pasting.

## If something's off

- **A blocked person still got a message.** Check that the entry matches the address they were sent to. An entry for a person's work email doesn't cover a different address of theirs.
- **I can't see the DNC page.** The Settings row is shown to admins. See [Roles and permissions](/workspace-settings/roles-and-permissions).
