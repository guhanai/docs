**Connect WhatsApp by scanning a QR code, the same way you link WhatsApp Web. Use it to send to prospects, to talk to Guhan from your phone, or both.**

## What you need

- A phone with WhatsApp installed (personal or Business).
- A minute to scan a QR code.

WhatsApp is treated as a personal channel. Use it for people who have already replied or agreed to hear from you, not for cold outreach.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Sender accounts">
    Settings, Sender accounts, then **Add sender**. You can also connect from your profile under Sender accounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick WhatsApp">
    The dialog shows how many senders you have used out of your limit. Click **Continue authentication**.

    <Frame caption="Add sender with WhatsApp selected. Click Continue authentication">
      <img src="/images/sa-dialog-whatsapp.png" alt="Add sender with WhatsApp selected. Click Continue authentication" width="800" height="580" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    On your phone, open WhatsApp, go to Settings, Linked Devices, Link a Device, and scan the code on screen. Codes expire quickly. If yours does, generate a new one.

    <Frame caption="Link WhatsApp: scan the QR code with your phone within 60 seconds, or choose a pairing code instead">
      <img src="/images/connect-whatsapp-qr.png" alt="Link WhatsApp: scan the QR code with your phone within 60 seconds, or choose a pairing code instead" width="525" height="870" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm it is active">
    After a short check, the sender appears in the list. A healthy sender shows no warning badge.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sending to prospects

Add a WhatsApp step to a campaign and choose this sender. The prospect needs a phone number on their record. If a prospect has no phone number, the step cannot send. You can reveal phone numbers from the prospect list. See [Enrichment](/audience/enrichment).

Every WhatsApp message goes out from your own number as a one-to-one chat. Replies come back to your Inbox.

WhatsApp has the lowest daily limit of any channel. See [Sending safety](/campaigns/sending-safety).

<Warning>
  Sending a lot of unsolicited WhatsApp messages can get your number restricted by WhatsApp. Stay near the defaults.
</Warning>

## Ask Guhan on WhatsApp

Open the account's settings and turn on **Conversation agent on WhatsApp**. Then message your own number from your phone. Guhan answers in that chat, with the same abilities as the in-app chat. See [Ask Guhan](/ask-guhan).

Only messages you send to yourself are picked up. Messages to prospects or contacts are never intercepted.

## Disconnecting and reconnecting

Use the account's menu, **Disconnect**, or unlink Guhan from your phone under WhatsApp, Settings, Linked Devices. If WhatsApp ends the linked session, the sender shows a status that needs attention and Guhan cannot send until you reconnect and scan a fresh QR code. See [Sender accounts](/get-started/sender-accounts/overview#sender-status).

## If something's off

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The QR code expired">
    Generate a new code and scan it again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A step did not send">
    The prospect may have no phone number, or the sender may need reconnecting. Check the prospect record and the sender's status.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Ask Guhan does not reply on WhatsApp">
    Check that the toggle is on for that account, and that you are messaging your own number rather than another chat.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
