**Connect Gmail or Outlook once. Guhan sends from your address and picks up replies in the Inbox.**

Connecting Gmail or Outlook also lets you connect the matching calendar without a second sign-in. See [Calendar integrations](/meetings/calendar-integrations).

## Supported accounts

- Gmail and Google Workspace
- Outlook and Microsoft 365

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Sender accounts">
    Settings, Sender accounts, then **Connect**. You can also connect from your profile under My sender accounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick Email (Gmail) or Email (Outlook)">
    The dialog shows how many senders you have used out of your limit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with the provider">
    Click **Continue authentication**. Google's or Microsoft's own sign-in opens in a new tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the permissions">
    Approve everything requested, including calendar access if you want to use this account as your calendar too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm it is active">
    Back in Guhan, the sender appears with an **Active** badge.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How messages go out

Every email is sent from your own address as a one-to-one message. Replies arrive in your normal mailbox and in the Guhan Inbox.

Emails that bounce are recorded against the original message and the sequence for that prospect stops. A bounce does not appear as a reply.

## Limits

Email has its own daily limit and sending window. They are covered in [Sending safety](/campaigns/sending-safety). Adjust them per account in the account's settings.

## If something's off

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Access denied from Google">
    Some Google Workspace admins restrict third-party sign-in. Ask your admin to allow Guhan, then try again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Calendar access was not granted">
    You can still send email, but the matching calendar will not work until you reconnect and approve every permission.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Re-authorize appears after a password change">
    Changing your password signs Guhan out. Open the account's menu in Settings, Sender accounts, and choose **Re-authorize**. Email steps wait until you do.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
