**Connect your LinkedIn once. Guhan sends invites, messages, comments, reactions, and profile views from it.**

<Note>
  Your LinkedIn account is used only for sending. Finding prospects never runs searches from your account.
</Note>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Sender accounts">
    Settings, Sender accounts. You can also connect from your profile under Sender accounts, or from the connect prompt when you set up a campaign's senders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add sender and pick LinkedIn">
    The dialog shows how many senders you have used out of your limit. If you are an admin with teammates, you can also choose who the sender is for.

    <Frame caption="Add sender with LinkedIn selected. Click Continue authentication">
      <img src="/images/sa-dialog-linkedin.png" alt="Add sender with LinkedIn selected. Click Continue authentication" width="800" height="580" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in on LinkedIn">
    Click **Continue authentication**. LinkedIn's sign-in opens in a new tab, with two ways to sign in: your **email and password**, or a **session cookie**. Choose one, as described below. Guhan never sees your password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to Guhan">
    Guhan finishes setup in a moment. The sender appears in the list. A healthy sender shows no warning badge.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Two ways to sign in

At the top of the sign-in page, pick a method: **Credentials** or **Cookies**.

### Method 1: email and password

Open the **Credentials** tab, enter the email and password you use for LinkedIn, and click **Login**. This is the simplest way. If it does not work for you, use a session cookie instead.

<Frame caption="The Credentials tab: enter your LinkedIn email and password, then click Login">
  <img src="/images/linkedin-signin-default.png" alt="The Credentials tab: enter your LinkedIn email and password, then click Login" width="512" height="578" />
</Frame>

### Method 2: session cookie

A session cookie is a small value that LinkedIn keeps in your browser while you are signed in. Copy one value from your browser, called **li_at**, and paste it into the **Cookies** tab. You do not need your password.

<Frame caption="The Cookies tab: paste your li_at value. The li_a field is optional, for Recruiter and Sales Navigator accounts">
  <img src="/images/linkedin-signin-using-cookie.png" alt="The Cookies tab: paste your li_at value. The li_a field is optional, for Recruiter and Sales Navigator accounts" width="512" height="585" />
</Frame>

<Warning>
  Treat the cookie like a password. Paste it only into this sign-in page and never share it. Signing out of LinkedIn ends that session, and the cookie stops working.
</Warning>

Your cookie has to be collected in the same browser as the sign-in page. Finding it is not possible on a phone or tablet app, so use a computer.

#### How to find your li_at cookie

This is what the **How to find them?** link on the sign-in page explains.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open LinkedIn">
    Open [linkedin.com](https://www.linkedin.com) in a new tab of the same browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Log in to the LinkedIn account you want to connect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your browser's developer tools">
    Press **F12** in Chrome, Edge or Firefox, or **Option + Command + I** in Safari. Then open the **Application** tab (Chrome and Edge) or the **Storage** tab (Firefox and Safari).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the LinkedIn cookies">
    In the left list, open **Cookies** and click **https://www.linkedin.com**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the li_at value">
    Find the row named **li_at**. Typing `li_at` in the filter box finds it quickly. Click the row and copy the **Cookie Value**.

    <Frame caption="In your browser's developer tools, open Cookies, then https://www.linkedin.com. Select the li_at row and copy the Cookie Value shown below it (blurred in this picture)">
      <img src="/images/linkedin-session-cookie.png" alt="In your browser's developer tools, open Cookies, then https://www.linkedin.com. Select the li_at row and copy the Cookie Value shown below it (blurred in this picture)" width="1405" height="853" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it and log in">
    Go back to the sign-in tab, paste the value into **Enter your li_at value**, and click **Login**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If your account has a Recruiter or Sales Navigator subscription, also copy the **li_a** cookie the same way and paste it into the optional **li_a** field.

## Sending limits and windows

LinkedIn is the channel with the tightest limits. Daily and weekly caps, the sending window, and pacing are all covered in [Sending safety](/campaigns/sending-safety). You can adjust them for each account in its settings.

<Warning>
  Raising limits above the defaults raises the risk of LinkedIn restricting your account. Keep the defaults unless you have a reason.
</Warning>

## Several LinkedIn accounts

You can connect more than one, up to your sender limit (see [Sender accounts](/get-started/sender-accounts/overview#how-many-senders-you-can-connect)). Each has its own limits and schedule. Add several to one campaign and Guhan spreads the work across them.

## If something's off

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The sign-in tab did not open">
    Your browser may have blocked the pop-up. Allow pop-ups for Guhan and click **Continue authentication** again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="LinkedIn refused the sign-in">
    Try the other sign-in method (Credentials or Cookies), a private window, or a different browser, and make sure you are signing into the account you meant to connect. If a cookie is refused, log in to LinkedIn again and copy a fresh **li_at** in the same browser.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says the account is already connected">
    Someone in your organization already connected this LinkedIn account. Ask them, or an admin, to reassign or disconnect it first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Stuck on finishing setup">
    Wait a minute, then refresh Settings, Sender accounts. If the sender is not there, connect again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The sender later shows Re-authorize or Reconnect needed">
    LinkedIn signed Guhan out. Use the action on the account's row. See [Sender accounts](/get-started/sender-accounts/overview#sender-status).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
