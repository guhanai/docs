---
title: Chrome extension
description: Add people to Guhan from LinkedIn without leaving the page. See who's already in Guhan, add people to a List, import a search, and enrich them after adding.
---

**The Guhan Chrome extension puts Guhan beside LinkedIn.** Open a profile, a people search, a company page or an event, and a panel shows who is already in your workspace and lets you add people to a List in a click. What happens next (messages, follow-ups, replies) runs from Guhan, as usual.

The extension only reads. It never sends a message, an invite, a comment or a follow on LinkedIn. Everything you send goes out from Guhan, through your [sender accounts](/get-started/sender-accounts/overview) and their [sending limits](/campaigns/sending-safety).

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Guhan to Chrome">
    Install the extension and pin it to your toolbar so the icon is always one click away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open LinkedIn">
    Go to a profile, a people search, a company page or an event.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Guhan icon">
    The panel opens beside the page and shows what it found.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your account">
    Click **Connect to Guhan** and sign in. You only do this once. If you belong to more than one workspace, switch between them from the name at the top of the panel. Your credit balance sits next to it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The panel's **⋯** menu has **How to use**, which opens a short illustrated guide.

## On a LinkedIn profile

### See who's already in Guhan

Open anyone's profile. Under their name the panel shows where they stand, so you never add or message the same person twice:

| You see | It means |
|---|---|
| **Not in Guhan** | They aren't in your workspace yet. |
| **In a list** | They're on a List and not in a live Campaign. |
| **In a live campaign** | They're enrolled in a Campaign that's running. |
| **Campaign paused** | Their Campaign is paused. |
| **Replied** | They've replied to you. |
| **Do not contact** | They're on your [Do Not Contact](/workspace-settings/do-not-contact) list. |
| **No reachable email** | They're in Guhan but no email address works for them. |
| **Possible duplicates** | More than one person in Guhan could be them. Guhan won't guess which, so it doesn't add them. |

You may also see **Strong fit** (or a weaker fit) and **Enriched**, and below that the Lists and Campaigns they belong to.

### Add them to a List

Click **Add to list**, then choose a List or create a new one.

- Lists that are fed by a search or a signal aren't offered, because those fill themselves. Pick or create a List you add to by hand.
- If an active Campaign uses the List, the panel tells you before you add. New people join that Campaign automatically, the same as anywhere else in Guhan.
- Adding someone is free. Your plan's active-prospect limit still applies, and the panel shows how much room you have left.

### Read the full profile

LinkedIn only loads a profile's Experience and Education when you scroll down to them, so at first the panel can't see them. Click **Load full details** and Guhan scrolls that profile for a few seconds, reads the current job and company, schools and top skills, then takes you back to where you were. It only happens when you click.

When you then add the person, the job title and company come with them.

<Frame caption="Experience and Education load when you click Load full details">
  <img src="/images/ext-load-full-details.webp" alt="The From this profile page card with a Load full details button" width="400" height="720" />
</Frame>

### Enrich them after adding

When you add someone, you can also choose to enrich them: profile details, work email, phone number, recent posts, a research brief, company details and company posts. Nothing is ticked by default, except that adding from a profile page ticks profile details and company details for you. You can untick them.

Nothing is charged while people are being added. When the add finishes, a card shows exactly how many people will be enriched and what it will cost, and runs only when you click **Enrich**. Costs are on [Plans and credits](/workspace-settings/plans-and-credits). See also [Enrichment](/audience/enrichment).

The contact section shows the cost before anything happens, and you're charged only if a verified email or a number is found.

## On a LinkedIn people search

Run any search, with any filters you like. The panel lists the people on the page with the same status badges as above.

### Import from a search

Click **Import…**, choose a List and how many people to add (up to 999), and Guhan goes through the result pages for you, a few seconds apart, the way you'd browse. Keep the LinkedIn tab open while it runs. When LinkedIn shows a person's current title and company in the results, those come along.

<Frame caption="Import from this search: you choose how many, and every person shows their status in Guhan">
  <img src="/images/ext-import-from-search.webp" alt="The Import from this search card with an Import button, above the people on the page with their status in Guhan" width="400" height="720" />
</Frame>

### Pick people as you go

Instead of importing a whole search, tick the people you want. Your picks are kept as you move through the result pages of that search, then **Add to list** adds them together. Start a different search and you start with a fresh selection, so picks from earlier browsing never carry into the next import.

<Frame caption="Your selection across pages, with an Add to list button">
  <img src="/images/ext-selection.webp" alt="The Your selection card listing two picked people, with an Add 2 to list button" width="400" height="720" />
</Frame>

### Watch it work

A progress card at the top shows how many people were added, were already in the List, or couldn't be added. Pause or stop whenever you like; everyone already added stays added.

<Frame caption="Progress for a running import, with Pause and Stop">

The same import works wherever LinkedIn lists people. In each case the panel opens that list of people, and you import from it exactly as you would from a search.

<Note>
  People from your followers and following lists are matched by their LinkedIn member ID. Someone who's already in Guhan under only a profile link may be added again.
</Note>

## What the extension doesn't do

- It doesn't send messages, invites, comments or follows on LinkedIn.
- It only reads the page you're on. It never opens other people's profiles to collect details.
- It doesn't use credits unless you choose to enrich, find an email or phone number, or generate a brief, and the cost is always shown before you confirm.

## If something looks off

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The panel says to reload the LinkedIn tab">
    The extension was updated after that tab opened. Reload the LinkedIn tab, then try again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The panel says to open a profile, a search, a company or an event">
    The extension works on those pages. Other LinkedIn pages, such as your feed or messages, aren't read.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An import stopped partway">
    LinkedIn may have asked you to check something, or the tab was closed. Look at the LinkedIn tab, finish anything it asks for, then start the import again. People already added stay added, and anyone already in the List is skipped.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
