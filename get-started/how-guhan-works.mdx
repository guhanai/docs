Guhan runs the same workflow a human SDR runs, in software. There is one flow to learn, and two nouns you'll meet along the way: **Lists** and **Campaigns**.

## The flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your business context">
    Fill in **Brand & Voice** (how you sound) and **Products** (what you sell and why it matters). This is the context behind every message Guhan writes.

    <Frame caption="Brand & Voice is in the sidebar. Its five tabs hold how you sound and what you sell: Brand, Voice, Competitors, Templates and Products & Services">
      <img src="/images/hw-brand.png" alt="Brand & Voice is in the sidebar. Its five tabs hold how you sound and what you sell: Brand, Voice, Competitors, Templates and Products & Services" width="1100" height="700" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import leads">
    Open **Prospects** and click **Import leads**. Find people with a search (optionally narrowed by a buying signal like funding or hiring), upload a CSV, or add someone by hand. Everyone is added to a **List**, a group of prospects you can review and point a Campaign at. As prospects are added, Guhan researches them and marks how well they fit.

    <Frame caption="Prospects is in the sidebar, and Import leads is at the top right of the page">
      <img src="/images/hw-prospects.png" alt="Prospects is in the sidebar, and Import leads is at the top right of the page" width="1100" height="700" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run a Campaign">
    A **Campaign** points at one or more Lists and runs a sequence — for example a LinkedIn invite, a wait, a DM, then an email. Each message is drafted for the individual prospect from your brief, their profile, and the signal that brought them in. Campaigns send from the sender accounts you connect.

    <Frame caption="Campaigns is in the sidebar. A campaign's Sequence tab is the editor: a LinkedIn invite, a wait for acceptance, a message, then a wait for a reply">
      <img src="/images/hw-campaign.png" alt="Campaigns is in the sidebar. A campaign's Sequence tab is the editor: a LinkedIn invite, a wait for acceptance, a message, then a wait for a reply" width="1100" height="720" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Handle replies">
    Replies arrive in your **Inbox**. Guhan classifies each one, and your reply rules decide what happens next: hand it to a person, draft a response for you to approve, or answer automatically. Meeting requests always go to a person.

    <Frame caption="The Inbox is in the sidebar. Replies are grouped by what needs you, each tagged with its next step, and the open conversation is on the right">
      <img src="/images/hw-inbox.png" alt="The Inbox is in the sidebar. Replies are grouped by what needs you, each tagged with its next step, and the open conversation is on the right" width="1380" height="800" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book meetings">
    Connect a calendar, and Guhan shares your booking link when someone is ready. Booked calls appear under **Meetings**.

    <Frame caption="Meetings is in the sidebar. Booked calls are grouped by day, with how each was booked">
      <img src="/images/hw-meetings.png" alt="Meetings is in the sidebar. Booked calls are grouped by day, with how each was booked" width="1100" height="700" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## An example

You sell an analytics tool to VPs of Marketing at SaaS companies.

- **You import leads** from the Prospects page with a signal source for **VP of Marketing** at **SaaS companies** with **50–500 employees** who **posted about attribution**. New matches are added to your List as Guhan finds them.
- **Your Campaign** uses that List and runs an invite, a DM, and a follow-up email, each written around the post that person shared.
- **You** open Guhan, check the Inbox, and take the conversations worth taking.

## Where you fit in

Guhan does the volume; you keep the judgment.

- **Review prospects** as they are added. Is this really your buyer?
- **Approve drafts** where you've asked to see them first.
- **Take over conversations** when a reply needs a person.

## What Guhan spends

Finding and enriching prospects, writing messages, and revealing contact details use **credits**. Sending itself is free, but it's governed by daily limits that protect your accounts. See [Plans and credits](/workspace-settings/plans-and-credits) for what costs what, and [Sending safety](/campaigns/sending-safety) for the limits.
