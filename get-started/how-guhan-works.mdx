**Guhan is an AI SDR. It runs the same six-step workflow a human SDR runs — executed in software, 24/7, at scale.**

## The SDR workflow Guhan runs

Every good SDR does these six things. Guhan does them for you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Learn the business">
    Read what you sell, who buys it, what problems it solves, how you differ from competitors, what your brand voice sounds like.

    **What this maps to in the app:** Brand & Voice + Products. You brief Guhan by filling these in.

    **Why it matters:** every message Guhan writes downstream is only as good as the context you give here. This is where quality comes from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find fit prospects">
    Continuously scan for people who match your ideal customer AND look ready to buy — hiring, funding, exec changes, post engagement.

    **What this maps to in the app:** Lists with signal sources. Each source has a WHO filter (titles, geo, industry, headcount) + optional buying signals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Research every prospect">
    Read the person's profile, role, company, and recent activity before writing anything.

    **What this maps to in the app:** the research brief that runs on every landed prospect. You never trigger it manually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draft hyper-personalized outreach">
    Write from scratch, per prospect, per channel — same brief adapts to LinkedIn, Email, and WhatsApp with each version referencing that person's real work.

    **What this maps to in the app:** the message agent inside every Campaign step. It reads Brand & Voice + Product + research brief for each draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Handle replies">
    Route "not now" replies to a pause. Drop unsubscribe requests onto DNC. Classify meeting requests. Escalate real conversations to you.

    **What this maps to in the app:** the Inbox. Guhan shows you only what needs your judgment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book meetings">
    Convert warm interest into calendar events without back-and-forth. Send scheduling links; sync the meeting to your calendar.

    **What this maps to in the app:** Meetings + calendar integrations + booking links.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The two building blocks: Lists and Campaigns

Under the hood the workflow above runs through two records:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="A List" icon="rectangle-list">
    A bucket of prospects. You feed it from search criteria, buying signals, competitors, another List, or a CSV — Guhan discovers new prospects daily and lands them here.

    Maps to workflow Step 2 (find) + Step 3 (research).
  </Card>
  <Card title="A Campaign" icon="paper-plane">
    Points at a List. Reads each prospect's profile, writes a personal message, runs the full follow-up across LinkedIn / Email / WhatsApp, routes replies to your Inbox.

    Maps to workflow Steps 4-6 (draft, handle, book).
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

They pass work between each other:

```
Brand & Voice + Products (Step 1)
  → List sources find prospects (Step 2)
    → List (research runs — Step 3)
      → Campaign picks them up (Step 4)
        → sends personal messages
          → Inbox routes replies (Step 5)
            → Meetings booked (Step 6)
```

## A concrete example

Say you sell an analytics tool to VPs of Marketing at SaaS companies.

**Your List** (you set this up once) has two sources:
- Search criteria: **VP of Marketing** at **SaaS companies** with **50–500 employees**
- Signal source: **posted about attribution** in the last 30 days

Every day, discovery runs against both sources. People matching either land in your List — with a Strong / Moderate / Weak fit chip Guhan's research agent assigns.

**Your Campaign** (also set up once):
- Pulls prospects from that List
- Runs this sequence:
  - Day 0 — LinkedIn invite
  - Day 3 — LinkedIn DM
  - Day 7 — Email
  - Day 14 — Follow-up email

Every message is written from scratch, referencing the specific post that person made about attribution — or whichever signal brought them in.

**You** open Guhan once a day, see who replied, jump into the interesting conversations. That's it.

## The work you never see

You don't have to think about any of this — but it's why Guhan actually works:

- **Reads every profile before writing** — so the message quotes their real role, company, recent post.
- **Throttles daily volume** to LinkedIn-safe limits — so your account doesn't get restricted.
- **Retries failed sends** — rate limits, network blips, temporary bans — so you don't lose anyone.
- **Withdraws stale invites after 14 days** — keeps your pending queue clean.
- **Classifies every reply** — meeting requests turn into calendar links, out-of-office replies pause the sequence for 7 days, unsubscribe requests drop them onto Do Not Contact.

## Where you fit in

Guhan handles the volume. You handle the judgment:

- **Review prospects** as they land — is this really your buyer?
- **Approve drafts** when Guhan asks for a second look on a high-stakes reply.
- **Take over threads** — Guhan hands you the conversation, you close it.
