**When a prospect asks to meet, you send a booking link from the Inbox. They pick a time, the event appears on your calendar, and the meeting appears on the Meetings page.**

A meeting request is the one reply Guhan never answers on its own. It always comes to a person, so you decide when to put your calendar in front of a prospect.

## Before you start

You need a connected calendar. Set one up under Settings, Calendars. See [Calendar integrations](/meetings/calendar-integrations) for Google, Outlook, Cal.com, and Calendly.

## Book a meeting

<Steps>
  <Step title="A meeting request arrives">
    The conversation appears in your Inbox under "Needs you" with a **Schedule** tag. Guhan marks these as high priority.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Send link">
    At the bottom of the thread. Guhan creates a booking link for you and sends it to the prospect in the same conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The prospect books">
    With Google or Outlook, they open a Guhan-hosted booking page showing your open times, pick one, and confirm. With Cal.com or Calendly, they go to your own booking page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The meeting shows up">
    The event is on your calendar and listed under Meetings, linked to the prospect and the conversation it came from.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also share your public booking link yourself, for example in a manual reply. Copy it from the calendar's row in Settings, Calendars. Bookings from it are matched to a prospect by email where possible.

## The Meetings page

Sidebar, Meetings. Tabs:

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Upcoming** | Booked and still ahead, grouped by Today, Tomorrow, This week, Next week, Later. |
| **Past** | Meetings that have already happened. |
| **Cancelled** | Meetings cancelled by either side. |
| **Unmatched** | Meetings on your calendar that Guhan could not tie to a prospect, such as bookings made outside Guhan. |
| **All** | Everything. |

Each meeting carries a badge for where it was booked: Picker (Guhan's booking page), Cal.com, Calendly, or Calendar (picked up from your calendar).

Click a meeting to see the prospect, the time, the join link, and the conversation behind it. To cancel, use **Cancel meeting**. The event is removed from the calendar and the prospect is notified by their calendar provider.

<Note>
  To reschedule, the prospect uses the reschedule link in their calendar invite. There is no reschedule button inside Guhan.
</Note>

## Meeting defaults

Workspace admins set defaults under Settings, Calendars, in the **Workspace meeting defaults** card:

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| **Default meeting length** | 15, 20, 30 (default), 45, 60, or 90 minutes |
| **Buffer between meetings** | No buffer (default), 5, 10, 15, or 30 minutes |
| **Default meeting title** | Supports `{firstName}` and `{lastName}`. Blank uses "Intro chat with {firstName}". |
| **Default scheduling tool** | Built-in picker, Cal.com link, or Calendly link |

Each rep can override their scheduling tool, and set their own working hours, timezone, and meeting room on their own calendar. The meeting length is fixed into a booking link when it is created, so changing the default later does not change links already sent.

## Teams

Each rep connects their own calendar. A booking link is created against the calendar of the person sending it, so the meeting appears on that person's calendar. Round-robin across reps is not supported.

## If something's off

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Send link says no calendar is connected">
    The person clicking needs their own active calendar. Connect it under Settings, Calendars, or from your profile under My calendars.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A booking is not showing on the Meetings page">
    For Cal.com and Calendly, bookings arrive through a connection you set up once in that tool. If it was never set up, or was set up incorrectly, bookings will not appear. Re-check the setup steps in [Calendar integrations](/meetings/calendar-integrations).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The prospect says no times are available">
    Open times come from your calendar's working hours and busy events. Check the working hours and timezone on the calendar under Settings, Calendars.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A booking link was shared by mistake">
    There is no way to revoke an individual link from the app today. If a public link has been shared more widely than you intended, contact support.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
