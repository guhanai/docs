**Connect the calendar you already use. Google and Outlook ride on your email sender's sign-in. Cal.com and Calendly use your booking link plus a one-time connection.**

Connect once under Settings, Calendars. Each rep connects their own.

## Google Calendar and Outlook

These need a connected email sender of the same kind first. The calendar uses that sender's sign-in, so there is no second login.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the email sender">
    If you have not already, connect Gmail or Outlook under Settings, Sender accounts. See [Connect email](/get-started/sender-accounts/connect-email). When you approve access, approve the calendar permissions too. If you leave those boxes unticked, the calendar cannot work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Settings, Calendars and connect">
    Click to connect a calendar and pick **Google Calendar** or **Outlook Calendar**. Admins can connect on behalf of a teammate. Teammates who already have a calendar are greyed out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the sender that hosts your bookings">
    Choose which email sender to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish setup">
    Confirm your timezone, which calendar bookings should go on (if you have more than one), your meeting room, and your working hours and days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  <img src="/images/calendar-finish-setup.png" alt="Finish setting up your calendar: time zone, meeting room and working hours" width="1440" height="900" />
</Frame>

**Meeting room options:** the platform default (a Google Meet or Teams link added to every booking automatically), Zoom (you paste your personal room link), or a custom meeting URL.

**Availability:** open times come from your working hours and days, minus anything marked busy on your calendar.

After setup, the calendar row has a **Copy public booking link** button for a link you can share anywhere.

## Cal.com

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect in Guhan">
    Settings, Calendars, connect, **Cal.com**. Paste your booking link (for example `https://cal.com/your-handle/intro30`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the connection URL">
    Guhan shows you a URL to paste into Cal.com.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it in Cal.com">
    In Cal.com, go to Settings, Developer, Webhooks. Create a new webhook, paste the URL, and subscribe to booking created, booking cancelled, and booking rescheduled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a test booking">
    Click **Listen** in Guhan and make one test booking. Guhan confirms it arrived and marks the calendar active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Calendly

The same four steps. In Calendly, go to Integrations, Webhook subscriptions, create a new webhook, paste the URL, and subscribe to invitee created and invitee canceled.

<Note>
  Guhan adds a hidden tracking tag to your Cal.com or Calendly link so it can match each booking to the prospect it was sent to. Slots, meeting length, questions, and video links stay under your control in that tool.
</Note>

## Which one should I use?

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I do not have a scheduling tool">
    Use Google or Outlook. Guhan hosts the booking page for you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I already use Cal.com or Calendly">
    Keep it. You get all of its features, and Guhan still records the meetings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Several reps">
    Each rep connects their own. A booking link goes against the calendar of the person who sends it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

You can set a workspace default scheduling tool under Settings, Calendars. Each rep can choose their own. See [Meetings](/meetings/overview#meeting-defaults).

## Reconnecting and disconnecting

If a calendar loses access, Guhan prompts you to reconnect. For Google and Outlook, reconnecting goes through the same sign-in as your email sender. If the calendar permission was not granted, Guhan says so and asks you to reconnect and approve every box.

To remove a calendar, use the disconnect button on its row. Meetings already booked are kept. If booking links from that calendar are still in use, Guhan warns you. Without a connected calendar, **Send link** in the Inbox will not work until you connect another.

## If something's off

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Google or Outlook is not offered, or says no sender is connected">
    Connect the matching email sender first. See [Connect email](/get-started/sender-accounts/connect-email).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Calendar permission missing">
    The calendar boxes were not approved during sign-in. Reconnect and approve every permission.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Test booking never arrives (Cal.com or Calendly)">
    Check that the connection URL was pasted exactly, and that the booking events listed above are selected.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
